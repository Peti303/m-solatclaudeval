LOGO RING - measurements from the screen recording (video px, 1280x720, t in seconds)
=====================================================================================

Count: 17 distinct badges (not ~20). Verified by ellipse fit at t=23.5s: 17 centres evenly spaced
at ~21.2 deg of ring parameter angle, no gaps/extra badges. Order clockwise (screen), starting at top:
 1 Coldy, 2 a.properties, 3 T5 monogram, 4 Khamovniki lion crest (shield looks like "12"/"17"),
 5 M mosaic emblem, 6 Show Me, 7 Horseka, 8 The Game, 9 Zorge No9, 10 Bolshevik, 11 A101,

(Coldy is the one hidden behind "The" at the very top at t~22.5s.)

Geometry (projected ring is an ellipse, axis-aligned, i.e. a circle tilted back in 3D; it is NOT a flat circle)
- Reference fit at t=23.5s (frame 236, 17 badge centres, residual <5px):
    centre ~ (651, 362) px, semi-axis x ~ 436 px, semi-axis y ~ 313 px.
  Leftmost badge centre x~192, rightmost x~1084 (badge outer edges ~135..1141); bottom badge centre y~667 (frame is
  clipped at y~712, bottom badges get cut off), top badge centre y~47.
- Ring centre x ~ 645 = horizontal centre of the headline (headline text spans x 285..1005). Ring is concentric with the
  headline block, ring centre about 50-80 px BELOW the headline's vertical middle; the ring is much wider than the headline
  (rx 436 vs headline half-width ~360), so the left/right badges sit outside the text and the lower/upper arcs cross over
  the text lines ("Work With Us" at the bottom, "The" at the top). Headline text is rendered ON TOP of the badges
  (white letters visible over white discs).
- Ellipse y-extent varies a bit over time (measured ry ~ 275 at t=21.5, ~313-330 at t=23.5-24.5): the tilt seems to
  change slightly with scroll; fits at other times are rough (+-30 px).
- Badge diameter depends on position (perspective): ~114 px (R~57) at the bottom arc (angles 120-230 deg),
  ~102-104 px at left/right (R~51-52), ~88 px (R~44) at the top (angles 330-50 deg).
  Rule of thumb: R(theta) ~ 50.5 + 6.5*cos(theta - 165 deg) with theta = clockwise angle from top.
  (NOT 50-70 px: at 1280 wide they are 88-114 px diameter.)

Motion while scrolling
- The whole ring translates UP with the page scroll (same scroll as the headline): ring first appears from the bottom
  edge at t~20.5 (only the top arc visible), ring centre y ~ 590 (t=21.0), ~475 (21.6), ~365 (22.5), ~340 (23.4),
  ~300 (24.5), ~150-180 (25.2), ~50 (25.5), < 0 after t~26; last badges (Ingrad/ERA/Coldy/Little) leave the top at
  t~27.5-28; footer ("Kononenko Architectural Bureau / Navigation...") then comes in (t~26.5-29). Scroll speed is not
  constant: slow ~t=23.5-24.5, fast (>200 px/s) after t~25.
- Simultaneously the ring ROTATES COUNTER-CLOCKWISE on screen: badges at the bottom slide left -> right (~15-17 px/frame
  at 10fps = ~160 px/s, i.e. ~21 deg/s ~ one badge slot per second), on the right side they go up, along the top they
  go right -> left, on the left they go down. Rotation looks steady (~0.37 rad/s) from t~21 to t~28.

  Consequence: a.properties, T5, crest, M and Show Me crossed the bright zone before/while the ring was still below the

- Opacity effect appears to be a plain colour/opacity multiply of the whole badge (logo ink dims too), badge discs are
  grey-on-black, there is no outline or shadow.
